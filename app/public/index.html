Good to know

Are you my lawyer? Is this legal?

No — Reclaim is a self-help tool, not a law firm or medical provider, and nothing here is legal or medical advice. You review, sign, and send your own appeal. That's your right, and insurers can't turn you away just because a tool helped you write it.

Will my appeal definitely win?

We can't promise that, and we won't pretend to. What we can say: appeals succeed far more often than people expect, and most denials are never even appealed. We give you a strong, caring, evidence-backed start.

What does it cost?

Your first appeal is free, and previewing your letter is always free. After that it's $10 for the finished, ready-to-mail letter plus deadline reminders, or $12/month for unlimited. Bill disputes only cost a share of what we save you.

Is my information safe?

Yes. Your records are encrypted, never sold, and never shared with advertisers, and you can delete everything at any time.
