Questions

Good to know

Is this legal? Are you my lawyer?

No — Overturn is a self-help document-preparation tool, not a law firm or medical provider, and nothing here is legal or medical advice. You review, sign, and submit your own appeal. That's your right, and insurers can't reject an appeal just because software helped you write it.

Do you guarantee my appeal will win?

No one honestly can. What we can say: appeals succeed far more often than people expect, and the vast majority of denials are never even appealed. We give you a strong, evidence-backed draft — the rest is your case on its merits.

What does it cost?

Your first appeal is free, and previewing your letter is always free. Extra appeals are $10 each for the finished, ready-to-mail PDF plus deadline tracking, or go unlimited with Plus at $12/month. Bill disputes are pure contingency — 25% of what we actually save you.

Is my health data safe?

Yes. Your records are encrypted, never sold, and never shared with advertisers. You can delete your data at any time.
