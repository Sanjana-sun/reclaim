Our mission

Nobody should lose care to paperwork

Fewer than 1% of insurance denials are ever appealed — not because people accept them, but because the process is built to exhaust you. We think that's the real scandal, and we built Overturn to end it.

Why we exist

Every year, tens of millions of claims are denied and hundreds of billions of dollars in medical bills go out wrong. Most denials that get appealed are overturned — but almost no one appeals, because it means decoding plan language, hunting for the right citations, and beating a deadline you didn't know you had.

AI changed the economics of that fight. A strong, evidence-backed appeal that used to take a specialist an afternoon can now be drafted in minutes — and handed to the person whose care is on the line, to review, sign, and send. That's the whole idea: put the tools the system uses against you, back in your hands.

How we make money

We charge patients only a little, and only when it helps — your first appeal is free, then $10 each (reading and copying your letter is always free), $12/mo for unlimited, or a share of what we save you on a bill. The bulk of our revenue comes from clinics, manufacturers, and employers who also win when a wrongful denial is overturned. Our incentives point the same way yours do.
