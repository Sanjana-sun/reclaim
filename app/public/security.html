Security & privacy

Your health data stays yours

You're trusting us with sensitive information at a stressful moment. We treat that seriously — here's exactly how we protect it.

Encrypted end to end

Your records and documents are encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is scoped and logged.

Never sold, never ad-tech

We don't sell your data and we don't put advertising trackers on any page that touches your health information.

Delete anytime

One click in your account settings permanently deletes your account and every appeal, bill, and payment record.

You stay in control

We never submit anything on your behalf. You review, sign, and send every document yourself.

Data-handling standard

As a direct-to-consumer service we operate under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, with breach notification and clear privacy commitments.

Responsible disclosure

Found an issue? Email security@overturn.dev — we investigate every report.
