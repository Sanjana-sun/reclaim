Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Plain-language template — have counsel review before relying on it.

What we collect

Account details (name, email), and the information you provide to build an appeal or bill dispute (insurer, denial reason, service, and any documents you upload). We collect basic usage data to operate and improve the service.

How we use it

To generate your documents, track deadlines, process payments, and improve accuracy. We use de-identified outcome data (e.g., which appeal types win against which insurers) — never raw personal health information — to improve the product.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your health information with advertisers or place ad-tech trackers on pages that handle it.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run Reclaim (e.g., hosting, payments, email), under contract. If we ever handle data on behalf of a clinic or health plan, we do so under a Business Associate Agreement.

Security & your rights

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can access, correct, or permanently delete your data at any time from account settings. Contact privacy@overturn.dev for requests.

Regulatory note

As a direct-to-consumer service, Reclaim operates under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and applicable state privacy laws.

This is a template for illustration, not legal advice. Consult an attorney before publishing a real privacy policy.
